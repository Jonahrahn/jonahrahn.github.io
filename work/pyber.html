01 Problem

A hypothetical ride-share company wanted to understand how its January through April 2019 revenue split across urban, suburban, and rural city types, to inform resource allocation.

02 Approach

Aggregated the trip and fare data by city type and week using Pandas, then produced comparative line charts in Matplotlib showing weekly total fares across the three segments over the four-month window.

03 Result

Urban accounted for $2,000 to $2,500 in weekly fares, suburban held steady around $1,000, rural barely reached $500. The urban segment is where incremental investment pays off; rural is a thin tail worth keeping but not chasing.
